Lorien is an open-source infinite canvas drawing and digital note-taking application, ported to Android by Oskar Gerlicz-Kowalczuk (Germaz Studio).

Features:
* Infinite canvas with smooth pinch-to-zoom and pan navigation
* Single-finger drawing with customizable brush sizes and color palettes
* Vector-based stroke rendering for crisp visuals at any zoom level
* SVG export functionality
* Clean, distraction-free interface optimized for mobile and tablet touchscreens
* 100% Free and Open Source (GNU GPLv3)
* Completely offline, zero telemetry, zero trackers, zero permissions required
